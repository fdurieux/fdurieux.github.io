# Known limitations and review priorities

This is the model author's disclosure, not an independent review verdict.

## Evidence and inference

- No production intervention dataset was supplied. The full cascade, the organizational loops and tool-specific DORA effects remain hypotheses. Evidence M, R, H and I in the model distinguishes bounded measurements, related research, hypotheses and inventory descriptions; mixed edge labels also appear and should be checked for clarity.
- The sequence-diagram studies have narrower coverage than the inventory. Results are reported from repository documentation; experiments were not rerun, raw records were not independently recomputed and all experimental material is not bundled.
- The with-author repair result adds useful evidence about resolving intent, with an LLM-author and disclosed limitations. The PDF currently emphasizes the author-less result. Review whether that selectivity weakens the argument or its balance.
- DORA capability research supports related mechanisms, not a tool-level effect size. NASA requirements management is guidance, not a quantitative measurement of the proposed amplification.
- The handoff contains exact pinned repository text, but only summaries and links for external pages. This is enough to locate the reasoning and source claims, not a substitute for independent source reading.

## Model structure

- No parameters, equations or time series have been fitted. Stock conservation expressions are conceptual bookkeeping, not a runnable simulation.
- Potential overlap between unresolved/invented intent, unresolved upstream defects, current knowledge and the U stock needs scrutiny. Separate causes, copies, defect counts, severity, workload and exposure before attempting measurement.
- Several edge conditions are broad. The reviewer should identify links that need explicit adoption, dependency, validation, exposure, timing or capacity conditions.
- The cascade figure selects and aggregates paths. In particular, the test-error-to-escape arrow compresses mediation through detection, and the DORA box aggregates metrics whose directions and boundaries differ. The JSON and per-metric page provide detail; assess whether the figure remains clear enough.
- Added cascade edges can duplicate explanatory paths in older loops. Structural graph checks do not establish that these variables are independent or that the model avoids double-counting effects.
- Feedback does not guarantee an observed dynamic: time delays, nonlinear thresholds, resource limits, incentives and power can change behaviour. No universal worsening or late-repair cost multiplier is asserted.

## Scope and completeness

- The source's covered/planned/open labels are programme descriptions at a dated revision, not a current independent audit of every third-party tool. Site and roadmap classify diagram-to-code drift differently; the model treats it as unavailable and discloses the discrepancy.
- Q4 performance and efficiency has no direct inventory mapping. Several other quality dimensions have weak or indirect paths. Review whether gaps are candidly explained and whether any justified pathway is missing.
- The nine systemic-design elements are mapped explicitly in JSON. The PDF spreads their treatment across several pages rather than providing a one-to-one table. Review whether this is sufficiently actionable.
- Stakeholder perspectives, participatory activities, governance and the service pilot are proposals. Actual priorities, acceptance thresholds, decision rights and organizational feasibility need stakeholder input.
- The PDF uses a SAFe delivery-wheel framing in its actor/lifecycle discussion. This is an assistant modelling choice, not a user requirement; review whether it aids the task or introduces unnecessary assumptions.
- Source updates after the pinned commits are outside this baseline. Compare separately if freshness is relevant.

Author-side checks cover PDF generation and visual layout, graph identifiers, references, loop connectivity and sign, inventory/definition counts, source-byte integrity and bundle contents. The verification record states these checks. It is not proof of causal validity, successful participation, production benefit or an independent review.
