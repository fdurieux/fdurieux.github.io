## 0. What v0.3 is

One revision, three sources:

1. the seven corrections of the out-of-sample review (pumllint #205,
   `REVIEW.md` F1–F7, verdict "fit for its stated purpose after specified

## 3. The extension: knowledge held by people

Boundary note first. The model is a model of artefacts, tools and
practices. v0.3 adds **one** source node outside that boundary, because the
dominant defect class is omission (C5) and omission has a cause the model
cannot name: the intent was never written because it lived in a person.
Nothing else from the people side enters: no node for the network, the
enforcement states or the succession plan, which are procedure
(`recreate_experiment/knowledge-transfer/DOMINIQUE-CASE.md`), not causal
structure.

| id | kind | definition | sign and target | label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| K1 `tacit` | source node (stock) | Knowledge of the system's intent, rules, exceptions and history that is held only by people: not stated in any artefact in the inventory. Includes the strong sense (cannot be fully told) and the weak sense (sayable, unwritten). Distinct from the existing `knowledge` node, which is the team's current understanding of the artefacts and is reduced by `drift` (E37). | — | H |
| K2 `tacit → updef` | edge | The larger the stock held only by people, the more upstream definitions omit what they needed to state. Mechanism: the author did not know, or knew and did not write. | + | H |
| K3 `tacit → unknown` | edge | What was never written arrives at the builder as an assumption. Mechanism: the builder cannot ask the person the kit stands in for. | + | H |
| K4 indicator on `tacit` | indicator | Two counts, both with a denominator: (a) assumption-register entries per artefact set (the C5 indicator, read as the downstream shadow of `tacit`); (b) the capture inventory: items found, validated, externalised with a check, residual by name. No percentage of knowledge captured: the population has no denominator. | — | M for (a), measured on five rebuilds; (b) is the method's reporting form, unrun |
| P9 elicitation | practice | Worked cases, counterexamples, exception walks, incident replay, quirk and orphan adjudication, decision history, with the successor present. Inventory from traces before any session. | `tacit` − | H |
| P10 externalisation | practice | Each accepted item to its normative home with an executable example or check: decision tables, glossary, process and sequence models, runbooks, decision records; the register links provenance and validation and is never a second source of truth. | `knowledge` +, `drift` − (through the change-process tie), `updef` − | H |
| P11 expert validation | practice | Every extracted claim labelled observed, inferred or intended; an expert confirms, rejects or adjudicates; a defect preserved for compatibility is a recorded decision. Feeds `detection` as the independent channel that breaks the C1 reverse path. | `detection` + | H; the channel's existence is M (the reviewer's verdict overturned by execution, F2's diverse derivation) but expert validation itself is unmeasured |

What would turn H into M: the departing-expert pilot's grading test
(questions in the set, answered correctly with a source, partial, wrong,
abstained, residual by name) and the before-and-after count of
assumption-register entries on an artefact set produced with and without
P9–P11. No edge from K1 or P9–P11 to any quality-in-use or delivery node:
the experiment never reached them (F9) and the pilot will not either.

## 6. Versioning, so that bundle, repository and sessions stop drifting

Layout under `model/` in this repository:

| path | holds |
|---|---|
| `model/model.json` | the model, with `"version"` and `"evidence_pin"` fields; the chat-library link removed, replaced by `"source": "fdurieux/fdurieux.github.io model/model.json"` |
| `model/CHANGELOG.md` | one entry per version; v0.2 (as reviewed, bundle sha256 recorded), v0.3 (this note, condensed) |
| `model/figure.svg`, `model/figure-knowledge-view.svg` | generated; the generator script beside them |
| `model/build_bundle.sh` | builds `dist/legible-model-<tag>.zip` with `SHA256SUMS` from a tag; the only way a bundle is made |

Rules:

1. One file is the model. Tags `v0.2` (the JSON as reviewed, link stripped,
   committed first so the review's checksum has a git ancestor) and `v0.3`.
2. The figure is generated from the JSON. The deck's reduced view is derived
   from the same node ids and cites the tag.
3. The bundle is built from a tag by the script, with its manifest, and is
   never hand-edited. pumllint's review folder keeps the manifest of the
   bundle it reviewed and adds the tag it corresponds to.
4. Evidence pins run both ways by tag: the model pins the pumllint commit
   (`evidence_pin`); pumllint's record names the model by git tag, not by
   zip hash.
5. Sessions work on branches and open pull requests; a session reviewing the
   model records the tag; a session changing it edits the JSON, regenerates
   the figures and the bundle, and leaves the merge to the maintainer.

Sequence: commit v0.2 and tag it; apply §1–§4 on a branch; tag v0.3 on
merge; rebuild the bundle if anyone still needs one; then the deck's figure,
citing v0.3.

## 7. What v0.3 does not do

- No magnitudes. Signs and evidence labels only, as in v0.2.
- No edge from knowledge capture to any delivery metric or to quality in
  use (F9).
- No modelling of the network, enforcement states or succession; they stay
  in the departing-expert method.
- No change to R01–R10 or to the systemic-design elements (R04), which the
  review did not test.
- Open question 1 of the review stays open: whether the reference is the
  intent. `fidelity` says which it measures (C5); the owner's rulings on
  J-F #304 and #305 decide the instances.
